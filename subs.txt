First, we can rewrite the constant 2 as a logarithm with base 2: $2 = \log_2(4)$. Substituting this into the equation gives $\log_2(x+6) = \log_2(4) + \log_2(x)$. Using logarithm addition rules, we combine the right side to get $\log_2(x+6) = \log_2(4x)$. Since the bases are the same, we can drop the logs and set the insides equal: $x + 6 = 4x$.
